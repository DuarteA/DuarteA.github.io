Duarte Antunes

Associate Professor

Control Systems Technology Group · Department of Mechanical Engineering · Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)

My research addresses optimal and event-triggered control, approximate dynamic programming, and reinforcement learning under partial information, with applications ranging from networked and robotic systems to precision agriculture and electron microscopy.

Six current research lines

What I'm working on

LINE 01

Robots for Precision Agriculture

Legged (robot dog) and wheeled ground robots for weeding and seeding in arable and greenhouse settings, building on our group's work on world modelling, decision-making and control for precision farming.

Legged roboticsPrecision farmingPlanning & control
LINE 02

Reinforcement Learning under Partial Information

Actor-critic methods for reinforcement learning with partial observability — including a “dramaturg”-style architecture that separates what an agent can act on from what it can only infer.

RLPartial observabilityActor-critic
LINE 03

Aerial Robots for Industrial Inspection

Autonomous drones carrying radiography payloads to inspect offshore wind turbine blades and high-voltage grid infrastructure without taking the asset offline.

Aerial roboticsMPCIndustrial inspection
LINE 04

Control and Learning for Electron Microscopy

Automating the tuning of transmission electron microscopes — learning aberration models from images, tracking drift as a dynamic process, and placing specimens precisely and repeatably.

Electron microscopyBayesian optimisationEstimation
LINE 05

Self-Learning Engines

Treating engine calibration as constrained Bayesian optimisation with an explicit risk budget, so an engine can keep recalibrating itself safely over its life.

Diesel enginesBayesian optimisationSafe learning
LINE 06

AI-Examined Learning

Using AI to conduct automated oral exams that verify students genuinely did the project work themselves — combining large language models with control-style verification and assessment design.

AI in educationAssessmentLLMs
